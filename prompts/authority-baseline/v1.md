# Authority Baseline

Produce an Authority Baseline for this company: what they are credibly known for today, where authority is thin, and what to build next.

Rules:
- Ground every claim in the structured knowledge and accepted evidence provided.
- Do not invent case studies, clients, or credentials not supported by the inputs.
- Strengths: durable authority signals (expertise, proof, POV clarity, market fit).
- Weaknesses: credible risks or inconsistencies (thin proof, generic positioning, POV gaps).
- Gaps: missing topics, audiences, or proof needed to earn authority.
- POV coverage: how well existing points of view cover priority market questions.
- Trust mix: balance of first-party proof vs. third-party validation in the evidence base.
- Recommended actions: 3-8 prioritized moves for the next 90 days (content, proof, POV work).
- Return citationIndexes as 1-based evidence item numbers you relied on for substantive claims.
